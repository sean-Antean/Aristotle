## 1. STRATEGY OVERVIEW

The Capital Flywheel is a cash-secured put selling system designed to:
- Generate 2-7% monthly returns per position
- Close trades early at 50% profit
- Recycle capital rapidly to maximize opportunities
- Transition to covered calls if assigned (The Wheel)

Core Philosophy:
- Sell puts on quality tickers during temporary pullbacks
- Collect premium as income
- Close early, never wait for expiration
- Stay in cash between opportunities

**v3.0 Update:** Leveraged products are now central to the strategy and are evaluated in a separate paper-only leveraged universe.

## 3. ENTRY CRITERIA (ALL MUST BE MET)

Before ANY trade, confirm ALL of the following:

[ ] Weekly EMA Cloud: 2/3 or 3/3 bullish (Fast: EMA 5>12, Medium: EMA 34>50, Slow: EMA 72>89)
[ ] Down Day: Stock is down -1% to -7% (ideal: -2% to -5%)
[ ] IV: Valid and available
[ ] Option Liquidity: Spread <= 10%, OI >= 25, Vol >= 10
[ ] Earnings: Not before expiration
[ ] Portfolio: Position size within tier limits
[ ] Cash: 50% minimum cash reserve maintained
[ ] Market Regime: MARKET_OPEN for actionable trade review

If ANY criterion is not met -> NO TRADE

## 7. RISK MANAGEMENT RULES

### HARD RULES (Never Break)
1. NO margin usage (cash-secured only)
2. NO position exceeding tier limits
3. NO earnings week trades
4. NO trading if cloud score < 2/3
5. ALWAYS maintain 50% cash reserve
6. NO autonomous trading — human approval required

### SOFT RULES (Flexibility Allowed)
1. Can close before 50% if opportunity arises
2. Can hold through minor dips if thesis intact
3. Can adjust strike if better risk/reward found

### STOP TRADING IF
- 3 consecutive losses
- Portfolio down >10% from peak
- Market in confirmed downtrend (all clouds red)
- Personal stress/emotional trading detected

## 8. EXIT STRATEGY

### PRIMARY EXIT (Preferred)
- GTC buy-to-close at 50% of entry premium
- Example: Sell put for $2.00 -> GTC to buy at $1.00
- Typically hits in 1-5 days
- Lock in profit, recycle capital

### SECONDARY EXIT (If not at 50%)
- Close at 21 DTE regardless of profit
- Avoid time decay acceleration
- Free up capital for new trades

### TERTIARY EXIT (Emergency)
- If underlying drops >15%
- Evaluate: roll down/out or take assignment
- Never realize loss if avoidable

---

## 9. ASSIGNMENT PROTOCOL (THE WHEEL)

If Put Expires ITM (You Get Assigned Stock):

### STEP 1: Decide
- Do you want to own this stock? -> YES -> Keep it
- Do you NOT want to own it? -> Sell more puts to lower cost basis

### STEP 2: If Keeping (Wheel Phase 2)
- Sell Covered Calls 15-20 DTE
- Target: 1-2% monthly return
- Strike: Slightly OTM
- Same 50% profit exit rule

### STEP 3: If Called Away
- Stock sold at strike price
- Keep all premiums collected
- Return to selling CSPs

### STEP 4: Cost Basis Tracking
- Track adjusted cost basis (strike - all premiums)
- Example: Assigned at $43, collected $2 premium
- True cost basis: $41 per share

## 14. TROUBLESHOOTING

### PROBLEM: No down-day candidates
SOLUTION: Wait. Cash is a position. Better setups tomorrow.

### PROBLEM: Premium too low (<2%)
SOLUTION: Skip trade. IV too low, not enough reward.

### PROBLEM: Assigned stock I don't want
SOLUTION: Sell more puts at lower strike to reduce cost basis. Or sell covered calls.

### PROBLEM: Trade going against me (down >10%)
SOLUTION: Evaluate rolling out in time and down in strike. Never realize loss if avoidable.

### PROBLEM: Missed 50% exit, now at 30%
SOLUTION: Close anyway. Profit is profit. Don't get greedy.

### PROBLEM: EMA clouds mixed (1 bullish, 2 bearish)
SOLUTION: No trade. Wait for clear trend.

### PROBLEM: thinkorswim app slow/crashing
SOLUTION: Use desktop version. Or call Schwab to place order.

## 15. INTRADAY TACTICAL PULLBACK MODE (Rev 1.4)

**Purpose:** Capture CSP entries on strong green days when a genuine intraday pullback occurs, without chasing at daily highs.

**Override Scope:** This mode may override **ONLY** the `NO_TRADE` (green day) tier. It does NOT override any hard gate failure (QQQ < 2/3, weekly clouds < 2/3, IV outside bounds, liquidity failure, earnings conflict, or sizing cap breach).

**Prerequisites (ALL must pass):**
1. Daily/Weekly Trend: ≥2/3 bullish daily clouds AND ≥2/3 bullish weekly clouds
2. QQQ Gate: GREEN (≥2/3 daily clouds bullish)
3. Universe: Core tickers only — TQQQ, SOXL, QQQ, SMH, SPY
4. Day Color: Underlying is green on the day (daily change > 0%)
5. Cash State: Combined cash ≥ 50% (target/moderate or higher)
6. No Immediate Re-entry: 24-hour block after profitable close on same ticker

**Intraday Pullback Definition:**
- Preferred: 60-minute candle completed RED (close < open) with pullback ≥ 1% from session high
- Secondary: 30-minute candle completed RED with pullback ≥ 1% from session high
- Completed candles only — no in-progress candles
- Pullback % = (session_high - current_price) / session_high × 100

**Grade & Sizing:**
- Grade: TACTICAL_INTRADAY-60m or TACTICAL_INTRADAY-30m
- Max Single Entry: 5% of combined net liq (half of normal 10% cap)
- Single tranche only — no multiple contracts
- All other gates (10% ticker, 25% IRA, IV, liquidity, exit-order) remain controlling

**Exit Rules:**
- Same 50% GTC profit target
- Same 21-DTE time exit
- Same emergency protocols
